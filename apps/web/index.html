Quorum on NEAR

A threshold group purchase. Buyers commit seats before the deadline; if the target is reached, the seller is paid, otherwise every seat is refunded with a share of the seller's bonus.

The provider is simulated, and prices are scaled down 10,000 times. The money flow is real: USDC on NEAR testnet.
